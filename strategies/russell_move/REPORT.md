**The long idea fails, and the opposite shows up.** Companies leaving the Russell 1000 did worse
than the average small company between the May rank day and the June rebuild in every one of

year's large losers and keep losing for a month (momentum), or the funds tracking the Russell
1000 and its style indexes sell them before the rebuild while Russell 2000 buying waits for
the last day. The selling-short side is planned below before its test years are read.
